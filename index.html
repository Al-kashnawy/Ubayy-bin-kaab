Our Namesake

Carrying a Name Built on the Qur'an

Ubayy ibn Ka'b was among the earliest scribes of revelation and the companion most celebrated for his mastery of Qur'anic recitation. Our college takes his name as both an honour and a standard — a daily reminder of the discipline, sincerity, and depth of knowledge we ask of every student who memorizes the Book here.

The college is organized into four categories — Tahfiz, Islamic Studies & Arabic, Western Education (Primary to Secondary), and Adult Non-Arabic Islamic Studies — opening for admission in stages. Tahfiz is open now, with the other categories to follow.

Our Purpose

Mission & Vision

Mission

Our mission at Ubayyu bin Ka'ab Memorial College is to prioritize genuine quality and high academic standards in Islamic education. While many tahfiz schools focus mainly on celebrations and graduating students without ensuring they possess accurate and adequate knowledge, we take a different approach. We train our students not only to memorize the Holy Qur'an and authentic Hadith, but also to understand, internalize, and practice the knowledge they acquire so that it shapes their character, conduct, and daily lives.

Vision

To stand as a leading model of Islamic-integrated education in Northern Nigeria, producing memorizers of the Qur'an, scholars of Islamic knowledge, and graduates who lead with faith and competence in every field they enter.
